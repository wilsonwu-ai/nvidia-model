# NVIDIA (NVDA) quarterly financial model

The Excel model behind my video "NVIDIA's model in two drivers": who pays each dollar of NVIDIA's sales, and how much of it NVIDIA keeps.

**Download: [NVIDIA_model_Wilson_Wu.xlsx](NVIDIA_model_Wilson_Wu.xlsx)**

## What's inside

Sixteen fiscal quarters, from the quarter ended 29 Jan 2023 to the quarter ended 25 Oct 2026, built from NVIDIA's earnings releases and SEC filings. Start with the README tab.

- **IS, BS, CF:** income statement, balance sheet and cash flow by quarter, with checks against the reported subtotals.
- **KPI:** revenue by platform, segment and geography, customer concentration, working capital and supplier commitments.
- **Guidance:** NVIDIA's past outlooks against actual results.
- **Forecast:** NVIDIA's outlook for the 14-week quarter to 31 Jan 2027, restated as an income statement, with sensitivity grids.
- **TieOut:** press release figures checked against SEC XBRL data.
- **Sources:** every filing used, with method notes.

Blue numbers are typed in from the filings or are named assumptions. Black numbers are formulas.

## Read this first

- The latest quarter (ended 25 Oct 2026) is unaudited and comes from the 17 Nov 2026 press release. Its 10-Q had not been filed as of 24 Nov 2026.
- The Forecast tab restates NVIDIA's own outlook. It is not my forecast. The profit-per-share grids assume a 17% tax rate, no other income and last quarter's share count.
